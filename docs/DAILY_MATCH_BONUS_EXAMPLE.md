# Daily Match Bonus - Example

## How Daily Match Bonus Works

Daily Match Bonus is earned when you have matching PV on both your left and right legs. You get **8% of matched PV**, paid in **$8 increments**.

---

## Example 1: Bronze Member

**Member:** You (Bronze rank)
**Left Leg PV:** 100 PV
**Right Leg PV:** 100 PV

### Calculation:
1. **Match PV** = min(100, 100) = **100 PV**
2. **Commission** = 100 PV × 8% = **$8.00**
3. **Matches** = $8.00 ÷ $8 = **1 match**
4. **Daily Cap** (Bronze) = 1 match × $8 = **$8**
5. **Actual Payout** = **$8.00**

✅ **Result: You earn $8 Daily Match Bonus**

---

## Key Points

1. **8% Rate:** All ranks get 8% of matched PV
2. **$8 Increments:** Payouts are always in $8 increments (rounded down)
3. **Daily Cap:** Each rank has a maximum daily limit
4. **Matching Required:** Must have PV on both left AND right legs
5. **Automatic:** Calculated daily when you have matching PV
6. **Triggers Matching Bonus:** When you earn Daily Match, your sponsor can earn Matching Bonus from your earnings

---

## What Happens After Daily Match?

After you earn Daily Match:
1. **Your PV is updated:** Matched PV is deducted from both legs
2. **Remaining PV carries forward:** Unmatched PV waits for next day
3. **Commission is created:** Daily Match commission is saved
4. **Wallet is credited:** Money is added to your wallet
5. **Sponsor bonus triggered:** Your sponsor can earn Matching Bonus from your Daily Match

---

## Example: Full Flow

**You (Silver):**
- Left: 200 PV, Right: 100 PV
- Daily Match: $8 (100 PV matched)
- Remaining: Left 100 PV, Right 0 PV

**Your Sponsor (Gold):**
- Earns Matching Bonus from your $8 Daily Match
- Gold rate: 40% of G1 Daily Match
- Matching Bonus: $8 × 40% = **$3.20**

✅ Both you and your sponsor earn bonuses!
