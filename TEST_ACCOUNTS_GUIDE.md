## 🆘 **TROUBLESHOOTING**

### Can't Login?
1. Verify phone number format (must include +999)
2. Check password (case-sensitive)
3. Ensure account is not inactive
4. Clear browser cache

### OTP Not Received During Testing?
1. Check backend logs: `tail -20 /var/log/supervisor/backend.out.log | grep "SMS OTP"`
2. OTP codes are displayed in console output
