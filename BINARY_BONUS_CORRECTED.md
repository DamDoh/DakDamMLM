# Binary Bonus - CORRECTED Calculation

## ✅ Correct Understanding:

Binary Bonus is calculated **separately for each leg**, using:
- **Left Leg**: Left leg volume × Left leg downline's rank rate
- **Right Leg**: Right leg volume × Right leg downline's rank rate

**NOT using weaker leg** - each leg uses its own volume!

---

## 📝 Example: RiThy VoNg (Silver Sponsor)

**Scenario:**
- **Sponsor**: RiThy VoNg (Silver rank)
- **Left Leg**: Member with Silver rank, 100 PV
- **Right Leg**: Member with Gold rank, 500 PV

**Calculation:**

**Left Leg Binary Bonus:**
- Left Leg Volume: 100 PV
- Left Leg Downline Rank: Silver → 10% rate
- Calculation: 100 PV × 10% = **$10.00**

**Right Leg Binary Bonus:**
- Right Leg Volume: 500 PV
- Right Leg Downline Rank: Gold → 14% rate
- Calculation: 500 PV × 14% = **$70.00**

**Total Binary Bonus**: $10.00 + $70.00 = **$80.00**

---

## 📊 Another Example: Gold Sponsor

**Scenario:**
- **Sponsor**: Gold rank
- **Left Leg**: Bronze downline, 200 PV
- **Right Leg**: Diamond downline, 1000 PV

**Calculation:**

**Left Leg**: 200 PV × 8% (Bronze rate) = **$16.00**
**Right Leg**: 1000 PV × 17% (Diamond rate) = **$170.00**
**Total**: **$186.00**

---

## ✅ Formula:

```
Left Leg Bonus = Left Leg Volume × Left Leg Downline Rank Rate
Right Leg Bonus = Right Leg Volume × Right Leg Downline Rank Rate
Total Binary Bonus = Left Leg Bonus + Right Leg Bonus
```

**Key Point**: Each leg uses its OWN volume, not the weaker leg!
