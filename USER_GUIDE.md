## Getting Started

### Registration

1. **Visit the Registration Page**
   - Go to the company website and click "Join Now" or "Register"
   - Or use a referral link from your sponsor

2. **Enter Your Information**
   - Full Name (First and Surname)
   - Phone Number (required for verification)
   - Email Address (optional but recommended)
   - Choose a strong password

3. **Phone Verification**
   - Enter the OTP (One-Time Password) sent to your phone
   - This verifies your phone number and activates your account

4. **Placement**
   - Your sponsor will place you in their network
   - You'll be assigned to left or right leg of their binary tree

### First Login

1. **Login with Phone**
   - Enter your phone number
   - Enter the OTP sent to your phone

2. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Add your email address
   - Upload a profile picture
   - Set your preferences

## Genealogy & Network

### Understanding Your Network

Your network is organized as a **binary tree**:

```
        Sponsor
       /      \
   Left Leg  Right Leg
    /   \      /   \
  ...  ...   ...  ...
```

- **Left Leg**: One side of your network
- **Right Leg**: Other side of your network
- **Personal Volume (PV)**: Points from your direct sales
- **Group Volume (GV)**: Points from your entire network

### Viewing Your Genealogy

1. **Access Genealogy**
   - Go to "Genealogy" in the main menu
   - View your tree in different formats

2. **Tree View Features**
   - Zoom in/out for better visibility
   - Search for specific members
   - View member details on click
   - See rank badges and PV values

3. **Network Statistics**
   - Total members in your network
   - Active vs inactive members
   - Network depth and width
   - Growth trends

## Commissions & Earnings

### How Commissions Work

DakDam uses a **binary compensation plan**:

1. **Binary Matching**: You earn on the weaker leg of your two legs
2. **Commission Rate**: Based on your rank (5% - 18%)
3. **Flush System**: Unmatched volume carries over to next period

### Commission Calculation

```
Commission = Weaker Leg Volume × Commission Rate
```

**Example:**
- Left Leg: 1,000 PV
- Right Leg: 800 PV
- Weaker Leg: 800 PV
- Your Rank: Silver (10%)
- Commission: 800 × 0.10 = $80

### Commission Payout

- **Frequency**: Weekly or monthly (company setting)
- **Method**: Direct deposit to E-Cash wallet
- **Timing**: End of commission period
- **Minimum Payout**: May have minimum thresholds

### Viewing Earnings

1. **Commission History**
   - Past commission payments
   - Breakdown by period
   - Tax information

2. **Commission Forecast**
   - Estimated next payout
   - Projected earnings
   - Rank advancement progress
