# How ADM001 Gets Binary Bonus - Detailed Explanation

## Structure Overview

```
ADM001 (Double President)
  └─ ADM045 (RiThy VoNg, Silver) - G1 Direct Downline
      ├─ ADM046 (Vong Boss, Diamond) - G2 Downline
      └─ ADM047 (Vong CEO, Diamond) - G2 Downline
```

---

## 1. From Whom Does ADM001 Get Binary Bonus?

### A) G1 Binary Bonus (Generation 1 - Direct Downlines)

**From:** ADM045 (RiThy VoNg, Silver) and their entire downline tree

**How it works:**
- ADM045 is ADM001's **direct downline** (G1)
- ADM001 gets Binary Bonus based on the **total leg volumes** from ADM045's entire subtree
- This includes:
  - ADM045's personal PV
  - ADM046's PV (and all their downlines)
  - ADM047's PV (and all their downlines)
  - Any other members under ADM045

**Calculation:**
- ADM001's rank: **Double President** → G1 Rate: **17%**
- ADM001 has two binary legs (Left and Right)
- The leg containing ADM045 contributes to one of ADM001's legs
- Binary Bonus = **Leg Volume × 17%**

**Example:**
- If ADM045's leg (including ADM046, ADM047, and all their downlines) generates **5,000 PV** in ADM001's left leg
- And ADM001's right leg has **3,000 PV**
- ADM001's G1 Binary Bonus = min(5,000, 3,000) × 17% = **3,000 × 17% = $510.00** (from the weaker leg)

---

### B) G2 Binary Bonus (Generation 2 - Second-Level Downlines)

**From:** ADM046 (Vong Boss, Diamond) and ADM047 (Vong CEO, Diamond)

**How it works:**
- ADM046 and ADM047 are **G2 downlines** (second generation)
- They are under ADM045 (G1), who is under ADM001
- ADM001 is **Double President** (Manager+), so eligible for G2 Binary Bonus
- G2 Rate for Double President: **3%**

**Calculation:**
- ADM046 (Diamond, let's say 1,000 PV) → ADM001 gets: **1,000 × 3% = $30.00** (one-time)
- ADM047 (Diamond, let's say 1,000 PV) → ADM001 gets: **1,000 × 3% = $30.00** (one-time)
- **Total G2 Binary Bonus: $60.00** (one-time for both G2s)

**Important:** G2 Binary Bonus is paid **ONCE** per G2 downline when they first qualify (non-Member rank with PV > 0). Even if ADM046 or ADM047 later increases their PV, ADM001 won't get additional G2 bonus for them.

---

## 2. How Many Times Does ADM001 Get Binary Bonus?

### G1 Binary Bonus: **Monthly**

**Frequency:** Calculated **once per month** during the commission cycle

**When:**
- Admin runs the monthly commission calculation cycle
- Or automatically at the end of each month
- Based on accumulated PV in both legs during that month

**Example Timeline:**
- **January:** ADM001 gets G1 Binary Bonus based on January leg volumes
- **February:** ADM001 gets G1 Binary Bonus based on February leg volumes
- **March:** ADM001 gets G1 Binary Bonus based on March leg volumes
- And so on...

**Each month's calculation:**
- Looks at all orders completed/delivered in that month
- Calculates total PV in left leg and right leg
- Applies 17% rate to the weaker leg
- Creates one commission record per month

---

### G2 Binary Bonus: **One-Time Per G2**

**Frequency:** **Once per G2 downline** (when they first qualify)

**When:**
- When ADM046 first reaches Bronze+ rank with PV > 0 → ADM001 gets G2 bonus for ADM046 (one-time)
- When ADM047 first reaches Bronze+ rank with PV > 0 → ADM001 gets G2 bonus for ADM047 (one-time)
- If ADM045 gets more G2 downlines in the future → ADM001 gets G2 bonus for each new G2 (one-time each)

**Important Notes:**
- G2 bonus is **NOT monthly** - it's a **one-time payment** per G2
- If ADM046's PV increases from 1,000 to 2,000, ADM001 does **NOT** get additional G2 bonus
- Only when a **new G2** qualifies does ADM001 get a new G2 bonus

## 5. Key Points

1. **G1 Binary Bonus is Monthly:**
   - Calculated every month based on leg volumes
   - ADM001 gets 17% of the weaker leg's volume
   - Continues as long as ADM001 has qualifying leg volumes

2. **G2 Binary Bonus is One-Time:**
   - Paid once per G2 downline when they first qualify
   - ADM001 gets 3% of each G2's PV (one-time)
   - Won't get additional G2 bonus if G2's PV increases later

3. **From Whom:**
   - **G1:** From ADM045 and their entire downline tree (leg volumes)
   - **G2:** From ADM046 and ADM047 individually (their personal PV)

4. **Qualification:**
   - ADM001 must meet qualification requirements:
     - Personal PV: 1,000 PV
     - Group PV: 40 PV (total team volume)
   - If not qualified, Binary Bonus = $0.00
